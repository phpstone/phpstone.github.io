Support

Find quick answers below. If you still need help, send us a report with your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of the issue.

Why can't I take a photo?

Open your device settings and confirm that Film Camera has permission to use the camera.

Why isn't my photo being saved?

Make sure Film Camera has permission to add photos to your library and that your device has enough available storage.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub. Please avoid including private photos or other sensitive information in a public report.
