Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Overview

Film Camera is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require an account and does not sell your personal information.

Photos and camera access

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you choose to take, save, or select a photo. Your photos remain on your device unless you explicitly share them using features provided by your device.

Data collection

Film Camera does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer's servers.

Changes

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Film Camera support page on GitHub.
